WHAT THIS TABLE SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
====================================================================================================

PPEF2  Absent in all four Rhinolophus. The best model is 48.9-58.4%% identity and is named
       PPEF1 by BOTH mammal reference proteomes, in all eight query-by-species combinations.
       Every species that retains PPEF2 gives >=81%% identity in 9-13 pieces, named PPEF2.
       The distributions do not overlap. Hipposideros armiger carries BOTH loci - PPEF2 at
       79.8%% over 687 aa and PPEF1 at 56.7%% over 318 aa - while R. hipposideros carries
       only the 57.6%%/318 aa PPEF1 signature, recovered by the same query in the same run.
       SAY: absent / not recoverable.   DO NOT SAY: pseudogenised (1 confident stops).

IL1F10 Absent in all nine rhinolophoids, including both megadermatids. The best model is
       34-47%% identity and is named IL1RN - the adjacent paralogue - by both references.
       Every other bat gives 56-84%% named IL1F10. IL1RN itself is intact in all 17 mammals,
       which is the internal control: the neighbour is recovered where the gene is not.
       SAY: IL-38 / IL1F10.   DO NOT SAY: IL-36. IL1F10 is IL-38; the IL-36 cytokines are
       IL1F6, IL1F8 and IL1F9, which were not tested here.

NOX5   Fragmentary in all four Rhinolophus AND all three Hipposideridae; intact in both
       megadermatids and every other bat. Only 6-26%% of the protein is recoverable, in 1-6
       pieces at ~71%% identity, against 80-96%% in 12-15 pieces where it is intact.
       SAY: fragmentary / not recoverable as an intact gene, in Rhinolophidae AND
       Hipposideridae.
       DO NOT SAY: pseudogenised. That requires disrupting mutations, and the clade-matched
       queries carry 2 confident stop(s) across all seven species. Do not say it is
       Rhinolophus-specific either: the Hipposideridae are equally affected.

NONE OF THE ABOVE  tests a phenotype. There is no expression data, no pulmonary challenge
       and no measured inflammatory response in this analysis. Gene loss is what was
       measured; a role in the fibroinflammatory response is a hypothesis the losses make
       worth testing. Stating it as a demonstration is the claim a reviewer will attack.
