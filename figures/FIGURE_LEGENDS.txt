FIGURE LEGENDS AND EXCLUSIONS
============================================================================

GENOMES SEARCHED BUT NOT ASSESSABLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Seventeen genomes were searched. 15 are reported. The 2 below carry ambiguous
  sequence (N) across the loci examined, so neither can say whether a gene is
  present there, and neither is reported.

  Hipposideros armiger       PPEF2 18,861 N (longest run 6,200 bp); NOX5 2,322 N; IL1F10 70 N
  Pteropus alecto            IL1F10 394 N (two runs over 100 bp); PPEF2 43 N

  This discards evidence that supports the argument: Hipposideros armiger
  returns PPEF2 INTACT and Pteropus alecto returns IL1F10 INTACT. Neither is
  used. A genome whose gaps make a negative untrustworthy cannot be cited for a
  positive. Counts are from results/LOCUS_GAPS.tsv.

EXCLUSION RULE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A genome is excluded when any locus drawn carries at least one ambiguous base
  (N) across the gene and 20 kb either side. The criterion is a property of the
  assembly, it is applied identically to all 15 genomes, and no call is consulted
  in applying it.

  0 of 15 genomes excluded.

POSITIVE CONTROL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ACE carries no claim in this paper. It is intact in 15 of 15 genomes
  shown. If it were not, no absence reported in that genome could be
  believed.
