METHODS (generated by PROMOTER.py from this run's parameters; nothing typed by hand)

Promoter sequences were searched for only at the locus where each belongs, located in each assembly independently: the AGTR2 promoter within the interval between the PLS3 and SLC6A14 orthologues, and the FBP2 promoter within 10000 bp upstream of the FBP2 orthologue. Orthologues were found with tblastn, chained into collinear models and accepted only when a blastp search of the model against the complete human RefSeq proteome returned that gene as the best match.

Each interval was read directly from the assembly and searched with blastn (task blastn, E < 1e-3, DUST filtering off). Collinear hits were chained and the total number of query bases aligned, counted once each, is reported; identity is weighted by alignment length.

A noise floor was measured per interval: the interval was shuffled 20 times preserving length and dinucleotide composition and rescanned, and the floor taken as the greater of the highest shuffled score and the mean plus 3 standard deviations. A promoter was called detected only if its chained score exceeded that floor. Intervals whose longest run of N exceeded 1000 bp were reported as not readable and no call was made.

The divergence at which this method would still detect a promoter was measured per interval by mutating the query at 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60% and embedding it in shuffled sequence of the same composition.
