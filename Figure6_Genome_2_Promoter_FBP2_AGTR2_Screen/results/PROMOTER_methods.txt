METHODS (generated by PROMOTER.py from this run's parameters; nothing typed by hand)

Each promoter was searched where the question required. The AGTR2 promoter was sought immediately upstream of the AGTR2 orthologue in species that retain the gene, and within the interval between the PLS3 and SLC6A14 orthologues in species that have lost it, since a deleted gene offers nothing to measure from. The FBP2 promoter was searched across the entire genome without any anchor: as a sensitivity control it is asked whether the assay detects a promoter at all, not whether one sits at a particular locus. Anchor genes were located in each assembly independently with tblastn, chained into collinear models, and accepted only when a blastp search of the model against the complete human RefSeq proteome returned that gene as the best match. Windows extended 10000 bp upstream of an anchor gene.

Sequence was read directly from the assembly and searched with blastn (task blastn, E < 1e-3, DUST low-complexity filtering and soft masking enabled). Hits were grouped by sequence, strand and position so that only one locus contributes, and chained greedily so that no query base is counted twice; the total number of query bases aligned and the length-weighted identity of that chain are reported.

A noise floor was measured for every search. For an anchored search the interval was shuffled 20 times preserving length and dinucleotide composition and rescanned. For a whole-genome search, where the subject cannot be shuffled, the query was instead shuffled 10 times the same way and each shuffle searched against that genome. In both cases the floor is the greater of the highest shuffled score and the mean plus 3 standard deviations. A promoter was called detected only if its chained score exceeded that floor AND its E-value was below 1e-05 AND at least 100 query bases aligned; the floor alone is insufficient, since a floor of zero would admit any chance match. Where nothing was detected and the longest run of N exceeded 1000 bp the locus was reported as unreadable rather than as a deletion; a gap can conceal an absence but cannot invalidate a positive.

Three controls are reported with every run. Each query was searched against the genome it is claimed to come from and must recover essentially all of itself at full identity, which identifies a sequence labelled as one species but taken from another. Every query was searched against all genomes of the opposite class, where roughly 300 million years of divergence leaves no alignable non-coding sequence, so any detection would indicate the assay returning a positive for sequence that cannot be present. The N content and longest N run of every interval searched are reported.

The divergence at which this method would still detect a promoter was measured per interval by mutating the query at 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60% and embedding it in shuffled sequence of the same composition.
